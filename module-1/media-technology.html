Media and technology: from the studio to the law

The eight ways media and technology shape art and design are arranged in rings, moving outward from the artist's studio to the law. Tick a ring to show or hide it. Hover over a word, or tap it, to read more. Then try the activity below to place the factors yourself.

Try it: place the factors

How it works: click a factor, then click the box below it that you think it belongs in. To move a factor, click it again and choose a different box. When all eight are placed, press Check my answers. Where your choice differs from the suggested box, the check shows the suggestion, so you can discuss why you placed it differently.
